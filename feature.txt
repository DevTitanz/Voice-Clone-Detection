1. CORE ON-DEVICE ACOUSTIC DEEPFAKE DETECTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- 100% Local Inference: Runs exclusively on the mobile device CPU with zero dependency
  on external cloud servers, preventing data interception.
- High-Frequency Vocoder Band Energy Ratio: Scans for neural vocoder synthesis artifacts
  in the 6kHz - 8kHz spectrum (HiFi-GAN, WaveNet, MelGAN signatures).
- Spectral Flatness Biomarker: Measures robotic phase noise and vocoder harmonic dispersion.
- Pitch Jitter & Regularity Factor: Detects unnaturally rigid pitch regularity (TTS artifacts)
  and vocoder micro-phase glitching.
- Zero-Crossing Rate (ZCR) Tracking: Identifies unvoiced-to-voiced robotic transition anomalies.
- Continuous Real-Time Risk Scoring: Emits a continuous probabilistic AI risk score (5% - 98%)
  recalculated every 250 milliseconds with 2.0-second circular sliding buffer.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. PROSODIC ACOUSTIC EMOTION CLASSIFICATION & FAKE URGENCY DETECTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Real-Time Prosodic Emotion Classifier:
  Analyzes Fundamental Frequency (F0), pitch variance (sigma_F0), and RMS dynamic energy:
  * Panic / Extreme Urgency (High volume energy + elevated F0 > 240 Hz)
  * High Pressure / Coercion (Aggressive volume + F0 > 200 Hz)
  * Agitated / Distressed (High pitch volatility sigma_F0 > 40 Hz)
  * Monotone / Flat Affect (Pitch variance sigma_F0 < 14 Hz with continuous speech)
  * Calm / Neutral (Conversational human prosody)
- Synthetic Emotion Incongruence Flagging (Fake Urgency Detection):
  Identifies the hallmark signature of AI voice cloning scams:
  The scammer's audio claims an emergency/panic, but the neural TTS vocoder produces
  unnaturally flat, rigid pitch variance (sigma_F0 < 16 Hz).
  Flag: "Fake Urgency Detected: Flat pitch variance with forced urgency shouting".

4. ZERO-DISK EPHEMERAL VOLATILE RAM STORAGE GUARANTEE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Ephemeral In-Memory Storage: Transcripts and acoustic frames are held exclusively
  in volatile RAM (StringBuilder buffer) while a call is active.
- Zero Persistence Guarantee:
  * 0 bytes written to disk
  * 0 bytes written to SQLite / Room databases
  * 0 bytes stored in SharedPreferences or cache
  * 0 bytes transmitted over network
- Immediate Purge on Call Disconnect:
  When call hangs up or monitoring stops, purgeMemory() executes immediately,
  wiping all volatile memory buffers to 0 bytes and displaying:
  "Volatile RAM Buffer Purged (0 Bytes Stored)".

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. AUTOMATIC INCOMING CALL DETECTION & FOREGROUND SHIELD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- System Telephony BroadcastReceiver (CallStateReceiver):
  * Listens to Android OS telephony state changes (TelephonyManager.ACTION_PHONE_STATE_CHANGED)
  * EXTRA_STATE_RINGING: Automatically pre-arms the AI shield upon incoming call.
  * EXTRA_STATE_OFFHOOK: The instant the call is answered, VoiceGuard automatically
    wakes up and starts screening without requiring user to open the app or tap buttons.
  * EXTRA_STATE_IDLE: The instant the call hangs up, the shield automatically terminates
    audio capture and purges memory.
- High-Priority Foreground Service (CallShieldService):
  * Operates with Android foregroundServiceType="microphone".
  * Displays persistent system notification: "VoiceGuard AI: Shield Active • Screening Call".
